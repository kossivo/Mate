Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

At Mate, your privacy is our core promise. Mate is engineered as a 100% on-device, local-first productivity assistant. We believe your schedules, thoughts, and calendar commitments belong exclusively to you.

1. Zero Telemetry & On-Device Processing

  • Local-First: All natural language parsing, scheduling algorithms, and time calculations run strictly on your device CPU/Neural Engine.
  • No External AI APIs: We do not transmit your thoughts or tasks to OpenAI, Google Cloud, Anthropic, or any third-party server.
  • No Tracking: Mate contains zero tracking code, advertising SDKs, or analytics trackers.

2. Apple Calendar & Reminders

  • Mate reads calendar events strictly in-memory to detect available focus slots.
  • Mate never alters or adds events without your explicit confirmation.

3. Private iCloud Sync

If enabled, your data syncs exclusively via Apple's Private CloudKit Database. The developers of Mate cannot see, decrypt, or access your data.
